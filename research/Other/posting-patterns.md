                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    # Posting Patterns

## Common Observations
- Most creators post consistently
- Educational content performs strongly
- Short-form writing dominates

## Formatting Trends
- Short paragraphs
- Minimal emojis
- Clear spacing
- Strong opening lines

## Engagement Techniques
- Asking questions
- Relatable problems
- Direct audience language
- Simple calls-to-action
