## Original Post (Cleaned)

I successfully broke every "rule" on LinkedIn.

After 360,000 followers, these are the facts:

1. "Post daily" — No. I've never posted every day consistently.

2. "Post minimum 3–4 times per week" — No. Sometimes life gets busy.

3. "DM your profile viewers daily" — I mostly message favorite commenters, not prospects.

4. "Leverage every trend" — I don't rush into trends. I prefer setting trends rather than following them.

5. "Share vulnerable stories" — Yes, but know your boundaries.

The key takeaway:

- Never force unsustainable consistency
- Skip posting when needed
- Build meaningful audience relationships
- Avoid blindly chasing trends
- Stay authentic without oversharing
