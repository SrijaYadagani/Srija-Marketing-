## Original Post (Cleaned)

AI is disrupting knowledge work and the economic premium on it is declining fast.

- Record layoffs for knowledge work
- Declining entry-level job markets
- Increased automation in law, software development, and customer service

And yet, there is massive social inertia pointing in the exact wrong direction:
just become a better knowledge worker.

The university system still promotes credentials.

The personal development industry still promotes more learning.

Corporate culture still rewards output and effort.

All of this reinforces outdated strategies.

The post identifies 5 major traps:

1. Work Harder  
2. Learn More  
3. More Credentials  
4. AI Augmentation Without Adaptation  
5. More Optimization

The key idea:

Stop asking how to become a better knowledge worker.

Start developing the capacities that AI cannot replicate.
