## Original Post (Cleaned)

Accumulating information used to be a competitive advantage.

Now, the advantage inverts.

The skill replacing it is clarity.

Accumulating information delivered real returns when information was scarce.

Now AI has:
- Infinite memory
- Instant recall
- Access to more information than any human could collect

The scarcity is gone.

When information becomes infinite, more information creates:
- More noise
- More competing frameworks
- More distraction

The challenge is no longer finding information.

The challenge is identifying:
- What is true
- What matters
- What is relevant right now

Information abundance without filtering becomes a liability.

Clarity is the ability to cut through noise and identify what actually matters.

Information processing adds.

Clarity subtracts.

In a world of infinite addition, subtraction becomes more valuable.

The premium on accumulating information is declining.

The premium on clarity is rising.
