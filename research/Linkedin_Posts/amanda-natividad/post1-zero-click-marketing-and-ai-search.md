## Original Post (Cleaned)

Zero Click Marketing is here, like it or not.

Google is rolling out what it calls its biggest Search update in 25+ years:

- AI Mode
- Longer conversational queries
- Follow-up questions
- Agentic search
- Custom-generated interfaces

This means:
- Fewer clean customer journeys
- Fewer obvious clicks
- Fewer attribution paths

What this means for marketers:

We still need to be discoverable.

We still need to create useful, credible, and specific content.

We still need to build a brand people recognize when ideas get:
- summarized
- cited
- mentioned
- repeated elsewhere

The marketers who understand how to earn influence before the visit, outside the visit, and sometimes instead of the visit will have the advantage.

If you're new here to the zero-click world, welcome.
