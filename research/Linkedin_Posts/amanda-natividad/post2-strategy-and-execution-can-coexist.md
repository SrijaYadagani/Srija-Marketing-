## Original Post (Cleaned)

Years ago, I realized I only wanted to become a CMO because as a smart, ambitious person, that's what I thought I was supposed to want.

More seniority.  
Bigger team.  
More strategy.  
Less execution.

My boss once asked me:

"Do you actually like managing people?"

At the time, I was exhausted and burnt out.

What sounded exciting instead?

Reading.  
Researching.  
Writing.  
Collaborating creatively.

Even now, that's what I love about being a high-level individual contributor.

I don't see it as "just executing."

It's a different kind of senior role.

One where I:
- Set strategy
- Execute ideas
- Own outcomes
- Solve complex problems
- Still care about details

At SparkToro, my role evolved across:
- Marketing Architect
- VP Marketing
- Chief Evangelist

And I still enjoy:
- Webinar marketing
- Graphic creation
- Audience research
- Content creation
- Getting into the details

Not every senior person wants to become a manager.

Not every strategic thinker should be removed from execution.

Some people do their best work when given trust, autonomy, and room to build.
