## Original Post (Cleaned)

3 months ago, I trained Claude to write in my voice.

I spent weeks building the perfect prompt.

I believed AI co-piloting would save time.

Then one day, I saw one of my scheduled posts online.

I was horrified.

Even though the writing was technically good, it felt empty.

It sounded like everyone else using the same AI tools.

I had lost:
- My voice
- My emotion
- My personality

The post argues that many creators use AI because they doubt their own communication ability.

But over-relying on AI creates long-term problems:
- Generic content
- Weak differentiation
- Reduced persuasive skill development

Key idea:

People consume content to feel something.

And nobody creates emotion like a real human creator.

The author compares AI-generated content to placing a barrier between creator and audience, reducing emotional connection.

Final takeaway:

Nobody can compete with you at being yourself.
