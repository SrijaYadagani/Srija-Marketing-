# Human Authenticity vs AI-Generated Content

Author: Dave Gerhardt  
Platform: LinkedIn  
Date Collected: 2026-05-21  
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/in/davegerhardt/

## Key Insights
- Human authenticity is becoming more valuable as AI-generated content increases.
- Audiences increasingly recognize and reject overly polished AI-style writing.
- Imperfections and personality create stronger emotional connection.
- Authentic storytelling can differentiate brands in crowded digital spaces.
- AI should improve productivity without removing human identity from content.

## Why This Matters for B2B SaaS
B2B SaaS brands increasingly use AI for content production, but maintaining authentic human voice remains critical for trust and audience engagement. This post highlights the growing importance of personality-driven marketing.

## Original Post (Cleaned)

We will place more value on the real. The authentic. The imperfect.

To know it was made by a human.

I'm feeling a shift.

We're using AI to get more productive, but something is missing.

We're humans, we're tribal, we take pride in making things.

For some, Domino's is the greatest pizza on earth.

But for many, they want the care and attention that goes into making something personal and real.

I really believe this.

I have been using my writing style, which is really just a disregard for grammar rules, for years now and everyone tells me how authentic it feels.

Even Claude tells me:
"That was a very Dave detail you added there."

I think human-made content is going to have an important place in history.

In marketing, I think this presents a great opportunity to use real people, real faces, and real stories.

Even if copy was not written by AI, if it sounds like AI, people notice.

So while you're all using AI to become more efficient, also find ways to lean into the human side.

Leave a smudge.

Have a typo.

Use words AI would never use.

We want it real.
