## Original Post (Cleaned)

Consistency always wins.

This morning we're sending out the 250th edition of my newsletter for Exit Five.

That's every week plus more since April 2023.

We've changed formats, changed email providers, changed writers and editors, but the one thing that did not change was the fact that the newsletter must go out every Tuesday.

A deadline is one of the greatest tricks there is for a creator.

Give yourself one — it's the guardrail you need to show up and keep showing up.

"I'm going to write this newsletter and it's going to go out every Tuesday no matter what, I don't care if anyone reads it."

vs

"Three weeks in a row I've done this, no one is really reading it and the results are low so I'm going to quit."

Same for the podcast we have.

Now 367 episodes and counting.

You certainly can get rich quick with content; sometimes the internet rewards your wit, humor, or creativity with virality.

But that's like playing the lottery.

The one thing you can control is the output.

It just might take weeks or years for something to pop off.

Here's to the creators out there who don't give up after a week.

Here's to the creators out there that don't care about the short-term results.

People might not read your stuff for weeks. For years.

But would you still do it?

I would.

Writing is clarity.

And so much good happens from publishing consistently.
