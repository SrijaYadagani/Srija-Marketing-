## Original Post (Cleaned)

 

The sharpest people on the planet are constantly improving.

 

Not to win some game.  
Not to build the biggest business.  
Not even to make the most money.

 

They're doing it just because it's fun to make progress.

 

Those 3 things above?  
They're all simply downstream of that.

 

But there's one big trick to self-improvement.

 

It's the word "self."

 

People can be kind.  
People can help you.  
People can cheer you on.

 

But nobody can do the improving except for you.

 

And that's at odds with a lot of people's notion that it's someone else's responsibility.

 

So who are you going to be?

 

The person who has the passion and curiosity to go out and improve themselves?

 

Or the person sitting around waiting for someone else to do the hard work that you won't?

 

If you're in the first group, I write a newsletter that examines self-improvement every week.
