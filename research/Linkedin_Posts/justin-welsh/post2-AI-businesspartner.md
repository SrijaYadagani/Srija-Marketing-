Most entrepreneurs don't have a strategy problem.

They've got a "what do I do next" problem.

What to launch.  
When to launch it.  
What to charge for it.  
What's broken.  
How to fix it.

The answer seems to change every week.

And without someone in your corner who actually knows your business, you're left guessing.

This is the exact problem Kajabi's new Cofounder product solves.

It's your new AI business partner built directly into your account.

It already knows your products, your offers, your audience, your pricing, and everything else you need to succeed.

You ask what to do next. It tells you.

Then it drafts the plan, the copy, the landing page, and the email sequence to get there.

But it also watches your business in between conversations.

If buyers aren't hearing from you after checkout, it flags it.

If your pricing page is screwed up, it tells you before you lose a sale.

These aren't just generic reminders.

They get set up using your actual business, numbers, and gaps.

That context changes everything.

Cofounder is the first of 5 products Kajabi has just launched.

If you've been waiting for a reason to build on Kajabi, this is the window.
