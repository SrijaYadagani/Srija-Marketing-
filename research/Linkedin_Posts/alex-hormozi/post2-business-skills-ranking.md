## Original Post (Cleaned)

The most valuable skills you can have to grow a successful business. Ranked in order of importance.

1) Product  
2) Marketing  
3) Sales

If you know how to sell, it's because you don't know how to market or you don't know how to build a product.

If you know how to market, you can sell a lot more but you are only going to sell it once because the product is not good.

If you have an exceptional product, you'll get more promotions from your customers than anything else and that is what ultimately scales the enterprise.

Paraphrasing a Naval quote I like a lot.
