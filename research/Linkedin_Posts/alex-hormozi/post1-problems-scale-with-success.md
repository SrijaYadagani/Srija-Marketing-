## Original Post (Cleaned)

In the beginning, your problems feel massive because you have very little to work with.

No money.  
No team.  
No experience.  
No leverage.

So every problem feels like survival.

But success doesn’t remove problems. It upgrades them.

The difference is that over time, you gain resources.

Better people.  
Better systems.  
Better judgment.  
More leverage.

You’re still solving hard problems.

You’re just no longer solving them alone.
