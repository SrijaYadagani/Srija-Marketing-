## Key Insights
- AI tools are not the problem; poor thinking and low-quality execution are.
- Strong creator voice remains important even when using AI assistance.
- Humor and exaggerated analogies increase engagement and memorability.
- Anti-AI positioning can become performative rather than practical.
- Personal writing style is often reflected in AI-generated outputs trained on creator content.

B2B SaaS marketers increasingly use AI tools for content creation. This post highlights the importance of maintaining human judgment, creativity, and distinct voice while leveraging AI for efficiency.
