## Original Post (Cleaned)

I’ve had a rough few weeks.

Business went sideways.

My daughter wasn’t doing well.

And when your child isn’t okay, your brain isn’t either.

Thankfully, she’s doing better now.

It reminded me of something from my corporate days.

I once told my boss I was struggling and needed a few days off.

He replied:
"Clients can’t wait because you’re not feeling it."

Years later, running my own business, I realized:

Good clients can wait.

Good clients care.

Good clients act like humans.

The author explains that none of his clients reacted negatively during the difficult period.

One client, Lara Acosta, responded with exceptional kindness and support.

The post highlights how empathy and humanity matter deeply in professional relationships.

The author also recommends Lara Acosta’s writing tool, Kleo, positioning it as both useful and built by good people.
