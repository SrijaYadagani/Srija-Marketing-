## Key Insights
- Consistent LinkedIn posting can increase visibility and opportunities.
- Personal transformation stories create strong audience engagement.
- Contrarian messaging improves shareability and discussion.
- Networking and audience-building are positioned as alternatives to traditional job applications.
- The post combines emotional storytelling with actionable advice.

## Why This Matters for B2B SaaS
B2B SaaS creators often use personal-brand content to attract opportunities, partnerships, and inbound leads. This post demonstrates how storytelling and consistency can strengthen audience trust and visibility.

## Original Post (Cleaned)

I got rejected from (nearly) every single job I applied to.

5yrs of Industry experience.  
2 degrees.  
But 0 offers.

The consistent rejections made me feel unemployable.

So, I started writing on LinkedIn daily.

If you're not seeing any luck either:

Swap writing resumes for writing posts on LinkedIn.

Quit waiting for opportunities and build your own.

Build an undeniable network that lifts you up.

1 month of writing on LinkedIn → 10x more visibility.

Lesson?

Become unemployable to others.

Build your own job instead.

Repost to inspire others.
